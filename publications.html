Sedentary Behavior and Eating Habits Associated with Fast Food Consumption Among Bangladeshi University Students: The Moderating Role of Residential Status

Faisal MA, Nurshad MO, Alam MT, Barua A. Second author of four. My roles: conceptualization, investigation, statistical analysis, data curation, drafting, and review and editing.
